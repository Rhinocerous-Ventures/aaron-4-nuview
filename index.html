About Aaron

A parent and professional committed to every student’s success

Aaron Gibson is both a parent and a professional committed to helping every student in the Nuview Union School District succeed. He holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Interactive Software Development with a minor in Psychology from Rochester Institute of Technology.

For the past 21 years, he has worked as a software engineer and engineering manager, leading teams, solving complex problems, managing resources, and making thoughtful, data-driven decisions. His daughter attends a school in the Nuview Union School District, giving him a personal investment in the success of the schools.
